Straight answers to common questions.

Are you the insurance company?
No. We're an independent lead generation service. We don't write the policies ourselves — we connect you with licensed insurance providers and their representatives who can offer you coverage.
Will the call buttons really dial?
Yes. Every "Call" button on this page dials (800) 460-9157 straight away on your phone, and opens your dialer on a computer. One tap, a real person in about 22 seconds.
What if I have tickets or an at‑fault accident?
Some of the companies we work with are good with drivers who have a rough record. Tell the agent your history up front and they'll point you to the prices that treat you fair — often well below what a big name quotes.
Does getting a quote affect my credit?
No. The quick comparison only does a soft check. You can stop at any time with zero effect on your score.
